PROFILE

About me

I am a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Reading, UK. My research focuses on distributed edge intelligence for IoT and data-driven systems, as well as energy-efficient and optimised computing in distributed environments.

My work contributes to next-generation intelligent systems and TinyML, with an emphasis on scalable, efficient, data-centric and agentic architectures. I bring over a decade of experience across academia and industry in Pakistan, Italy and the United Kingdom, contributing to research projects, curriculum development, module delivery and undergraduate and postgraduate dissertation supervision.

I have authored more than 15 peer-reviewed publications in international journals and conferences. I also serve on technical programme committees, review for international journals and contribute to the AI-INTERVENE doctoral training programme.
